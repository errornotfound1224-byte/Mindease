Understanding ADHD

ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder) is a neurodevelopmental condition affecting attention, impulse control, and activity levels.

Professional Diagnosis: Only qualified healthcare professionals can diagnose ADHD through comprehensive evaluation including clinical interviews, observations, and standardized assessments.

When to Seek Professional Help

Consider consulting a healthcare professional if:

  • Attention difficulties significantly affect school, work, or relationships
  • Symptoms have persisted for six months or longer
  • Multiple areas of life are impacted
  • You want a comprehensive evaluation and treatment options
