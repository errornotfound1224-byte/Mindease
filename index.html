Important: MindEase is a Wellness Tool, Not a Diagnostic Service

MindEase helps you explore patterns and build healthy habits, but it cannot diagnose ADHD or any other condition. Our questionnaire is an exploratory screening tool, not a clinical assessment.

If attention-related difficulties are affecting your daily life, we encourage you to speak with a qualified healthcare professional who can provide a comprehensive evaluation.
